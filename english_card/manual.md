# English Learning Suite (English Card)

## Overview
The **English Learning Suite** is a comprehensive collection of 16 interactive applications tailored for mastering English vocabulary, grammar, and syntax. Each module focuses on a specific aspect of the language, utilizing spaced repetition, visual syntax trees, and gamified quizzes to accelerate learning.

## Modules

### 1. English Flashcards & Quizzes
- **English Card**: Traditional flashcards for vocabulary memorization.
- **English Quiz**: Multiple-choice vocabulary testing.
- **English Word App**: An expanded vocabulary trainer with word examples.
- **Countable Nouns**: Specifically drills the distinction between countable and uncountable nouns.

### 2. Grammar & Structure
- **Grammar Basic & High School**: Step-by-step interactive lessons covering foundational to advanced grammar rules.
- **Grammar Structure**: Visualizes how sentences are built, breaking them into Subjects, Verbs, Objects, and Compliments (SVOC).
- **Grammar Verb & Term**: Focused drills on verb conjugations and identifying grammatical terminology.

### 3. Syntax Parsing
- **Syntax as Code**: A unique approach that treats English sentence structure like programming syntax, encouraging analytical reading.
- **Syntax Parser**: Allows you to input custom sentences and visually parses them into their grammatical components.

### 4. Modifiers & Puzzles
- **Modifiers**: Lessons identifying adjectives, adverbs, and complex modifier phrases.
- **No M Sentence & No M Puzzle**: Challenges users to identify the "core" sentence by stripping away all modifiers (M). 
- **Grammar Puzzle & Grammar Train**: Gamified puzzle interfaces (including a train-map UI) for dragging and dropping grammatical components into correct sequences.

## How to Use
1. **Select a Module**: Open the desired app from the "Data & Language" section of the main portal.
2. **Interactive Elements**: Use your mouse or touch screen to drag words, select options, or flip flashcards.
3. **Progress**: The modules are largely self-contained. For quizzes, your score and streak will be displayed on the screen. 
4. **Reset/Navigation**: Use the provided on-screen buttons (often styled like macOS window controls) to restart a lesson or return to the main menu.
